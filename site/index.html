Sunday Drive

Support

Sunday Drive is a free iPhone app (iOS 17 or later) that plans the scenic way to somewhere, or a loop from where you are, in the six New England states: Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island and Vermont. Every road is scored for scenery from open data. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it is open source.

Common questions

It says the point is outside the covered road network

Sunday Drive covers New England only. From anywhere, you can type a New England town as your start. Loop and My Location only work when you are in New England.

It asks for Precise Location

Turn-by-turn guidance needs your precise position. With Precise Location off, starting a drive asks to use it for that drive. If you decline, the app cannot navigate, and it says so. You can turn Precise Location on in Settings, under Sunday Drive → Location.

Does it work offline, on CarPlay, or on Android?

No. Routes are computed on our routing server, so the app needs a data connection. There is no CarPlay or Android version.

Safety

Mount your phone, and don't handle it while you drive. Some roads close for part of the year and the app may not know, so follow posted signs and closures.
