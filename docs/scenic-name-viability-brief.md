## 1. Why this is being asked now, and what it would cost to act on

The owner is **lukewarm on "Victory Lap"** — recorded at the time the name was
chosen — and the word "scenic" is still the most accurate description of what
this product does. So the question is live even though the rename shipped.

**The reversal window is open and this is the fact that makes the research worth
doing.** Verified against App Store Connect's own reference on 2026-09-20:

- The **bundle identifier is permanent at the first build upload, TestFlight
  included** — not at App Review, which is how this project's docs used to
  phrase it. **Nothing has been uploaded.** So `app.victorylap` is still free to
  change.
- The **app name is not permanent at all** — editable until first submission to
  App Review, and changeable with any later version after that.
- The **SKU** is fixed at app-record creation. No record exists yet.

So the cost of reverting is the engineering cost of the rename, roughly what it
cost to do: **114 machine-countable identifiers**, an afternoon, done by reading
every hit. It is not blocked by anything external. State this plainly in the
findings so the decision is priced honestly — the answer "Scenic is viable" is
actionable today and will stop being actionable at the first TestFlight upload.

## 2. What is already settled — do not re-derive any of it

The bare word has been screened **twice, independently**, and both screens
agree. Read [`victory-lap-naming.md`](victory-lap-naming.md) §5–§6 and
[`trademark-knockout-findings.md`](trademark-knockout-findings.md) §1. The
findings, so you can start from them:

**App Store leg — this is the binding one.**

- `itunes.apple.com/search?term=Scenic&entity=software&country=us&limit=200`
  → 157 results, **43 with "scenic" in the app NAME, of which 25 sit in
  Navigation (14) or Travel (11)**. Counted programmatically. An earlier
  eyeballed count said six; see Trap 3.
- **Scenic — Motorcycle Navigation** (Applified Life Ltd., Navigation,
  2016-07-21, `scenic.app`): ~30M hours ridden, 200k+ user routes, 4.7★, paid
  Premium tier. **Identical mark, identical goods, senior user** — the worst
  pairing there is.
- **"Scenic Route" is already a shipping bundle identifier in Navigation,
  twice**: `uk.co.turnkeyit.ScenicRoute` (*Scenic Way*, 2026-07-18) and
  `uk.co.scenicroutes` (*Scenic Routes: Walk & Transit*, 2026-06-17).
- A separate query, `term=scenic route`, returns 157 results and **zero** apps
  with *"the scenic route"* in the name. The definite article is the only part
  of that phrase nobody has taken.

**Register leg.**

- `SCENIC`, serial **79431580**, Scenic Tours Europe AG (Switzerland), a live
  §66(a) Madrid designation covering **IC 009, 039 and 042** — this product's
  own classes. Status as of 2026-09-20: **NON-FINAL ACTION – MAILED**, i.e.
  under examination. If it registers, the exact word becomes a live US
  registration in this field, held by a travel company.
- The word is **merely descriptive** for an app that finds scenic routes →
  Lanham §2(e)(1). Likely refused on the Principal Register absent acquired
  distinctiveness, and weak even if registered.

**Product leg.** 25 apps in the two relevant categories means search burial.
That is a discoverability argument, independent of law, and it does not go away
if the legal picture improves.

**The standing conclusion these produced:** keeping "Scenic" is the
highest-risk naming option available. Your job is not to re-confirm that. It is
§3.

## 3. What is actually open

Two things, and only two.

### 3a. Has serial 79431580 moved?

It was under examination on 2026-09-20 with a non-final office action mailed.
Check its current status. **If it registers**, the register argument against
"Scenic" hardens. **If it is abandoned**, that argument disappears entirely and
the App Store argument stands alone — which changes the balance, because the App
Store argument is about a complaint rather than a court.

### 3b. No variation of "Scenic" has ever been screened. Not one.

Eight names have been screened to full depth — Camber, Aimless, Detourist,
Longcut, Backway, Sideroad, Sunday Drive, Victory Lap. **Every one of them
avoids the word.** Nobody has ever asked whether a mutation of "scenic" clears.

That is the real work here. Enumerate a candidate set and screen each to the
same depth as `trademark-knockout-findings.md` §12–§15.

**The structural argument that should shape your candidate set, offered as a
hypothesis to test rather than a conclusion to adopt:** the problem with
"Scenic" is *descriptiveness*, and the obvious variations make it worse rather
than better. "Scenic Route", "Scenic Drive", "Scenic Way", "Scenic Roads" are
all **more** descriptive than the bare adjective, not less — and two of them are
already shipping bundle identifiers in Navigation (§2). If any variation works,
it is one that stops being the dictionary word: a coinage, a compound where
"scenic" is not the head noun, a clipping, a respelling, or a portmanteau.
Whether such a thing can be *pronounceable and not embarrassing* is the part
that cannot be settled from the register, and you should say so if the answer is
no.

Generate the candidates yourself — you will think of better ones than a list
written here would contain. Cover at minimum the obvious descriptive compounds
(so the finding is recorded rather than assumed), the coinage space, and any
variation the owner might reasonably propose next so he is not sent back for
another round.

## 4. The two bars, and which one binds

A name must clear **both**. Judge every candidate against both and say which
one, if either, it fails.

1. **The legal bar** — federal register plus common-law use. What this catches
   is a conflict someone could assert.
2. **The App Store bar** — Apple's guideline **2.3.7** ("App subtitles … should
   not … reference other apps") and **5.2.1** (copycat names and metadata).
   This is **Apple's rule, not trademark law**, and it is enforced by complaint.

**For a free app with no revenue the second bar is the binding one**, and this
is already settled project reasoning ([`release-plan.md`](release-plan.md)
Decision 2): the realistic failure mode is not litigation — that costs a
complainant more than it recovers — it is an **App Store complaint, which is
cheap to file and can pull an app pending resolution**. A name that is legally
defensible and hands a competitor an easy complaint form is not a good name.

## 6. Traps

1. **A clean register does not clear a name, and this project has the
   measurement that proves it.** Run as a positive control, the federal register
   **missed Scenic — Motorcycle Navigation entirely**: Applified Life Ltd. holds
   no US trademark record at all. The single most important conflict in this
   whole question is invisible on the register leg. **Always run both legs, and
   never report a register-only result as a verdict.**
2. **Verify a wildcard returns non-zero before believing any zero.** Known
   controls on the `wordmark` field: `*scenic*` → 417, `*cut*` → 26,266. A zero
   from an untested query shape is not evidence.
3. **Count App Store hits in code, never by reading the ranked list.** This has
   now failed twice on this exact question — `branding-brainstorm.md` §3 said
   six Navigation/Travel apps contain "scenic"; programmatic counting against
   the *name* field said **25**. Eyeballing a ranked list stops early. Use
   `limit=200` and filter on the name field.
4. **Do not touch the 158 correct-English uses of "scenic".** *Scenic route*,
   *scenic score*, *scenic km*, *scenic arm*, *scenic byway* are the product's
   vocabulary and stay whatever the branding answer is — the word is
   descriptive, which is simultaneously why it fails as a mark and why it
   belongs in the domain language. This task is about the **mark**, not the
   vocabulary. Related: `docs/route-census/` holds 1,967 uses of the word as a
   *data column value*. Never find-and-replace.
5. **Do not spawn research sub-agents.** Broad general-purpose agents on this
   project have silently fanned out — two became six, over a million tokens —
   and exhausted the owner's session limit mid-task. Do the searching yourself,
   with the browser and direct fetches.
6. **`docs/README.md` will conflict.** Another dispatched session is adding rows
   to the same index table right now. It is a pure add/add append — keep both
   rows, do not take a side. This has happened twice today already.
7. **Do not recommend a name.** Give each candidate a verdict with its evidence
   and say which bar it fails. The choice is the owner's, he has already been
   through one naming round, and a brief that picks for him will get re-argued
   rather than used.
